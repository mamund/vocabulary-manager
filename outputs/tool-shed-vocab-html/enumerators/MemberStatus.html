MemberStatus

Kind: Enumerator

The recognized participation statuses of a Member.

Values

ValueDefinition
activeThe Member may participate in borrowing, subject to borrowing rules.
inactiveThe Member remains on file but cannot reserve or check out Tools.

Values belong to this enumerator and do not have independent vocabulary pages.
